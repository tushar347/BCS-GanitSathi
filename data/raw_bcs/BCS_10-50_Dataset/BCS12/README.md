# 12th BCS (Police) GonitSathi Math JSON

Main in-scope questions: 11
Review required: 1
Excluded from initial six-topic benchmark: 5
Authored controlled histories: 44

## Scope
Initial benchmark groups: percentages/profit-loss; ratios/proportions; work/time; speed/distance; averages/mixtures; elementary algebra/number relations.

## Data provenance
Question wording, choices and printed keys are transcribed from the user-provided PDF. Solutions, mistake scenarios, diagnoses and probes are model-authored drafts. They are not real student histories and must be independently reviewed before gold/test use.

## Quality rule
Malformed, mathematically inconsistent or insufficiently specified in-scope source items are withheld in `review_required.json` rather than repaired silently. Geometry, mensuration, set/spatial/other reasoning outside the six initial groups is logged in `excluded_out_of_scope.json` where relevant.

## Folders
- compatibility_demo_format/: question/answer files similar to the earlier collection format
- research_benchmark/: problem, solution, history, diagnosis/probe and output-policy layers
- review_required.json: defective/ambiguous in-scope items withheld from main data
- excluded_out_of_scope.json: math/reasoning items outside the initial six topic groups
