# Expert Evaluation Rubrics & Anchors (Appendix B.3)

Human audit evaluations (§13.1) must follow these anchored 3-point scoring scales (0, 1, 2). All evaluations must be performed by 2 independent adjudicators with conflicts settled by an expert arbitrator.

| Dimension | Score 0 (Deficient) | Score 1 (Marginal / Acceptable) | Score 2 (Exemplary) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Mathematical Correctness** | Mathematically incorrect, invalid calculation, or misleading algebraic premise. | Partially correct, or correct calculation with insufficient supporting steps/assumptions. | Mathematically sound, fully verified within problem domain and declared assumptions. |
| **2. Evidence Alignment** | Diagnosis or pedagogical action is unsupported by the student's recorded attempt history. | Plausible inference, but asserts too strong a claim (overconfident) or too weak (evasive). | Precisely calibrated to observed student evidence; distinguishes slips from misconceptions. |
| **3. Targeting** | Misses the relevant difficulty; targets an irrelevant or already-mastered sub-step. | Addresses a related issue, but misses the earliest or most decisive root error. | Directly targets the earliest unaddressed error or pivotal misconception. |
| **4. Disclosure Policy** | Prohibited decisive disclosure (leaks final answer, equation, or crucial intermediate step). | Borderline disclosure; provides excessive guidance that diminishes productive struggle. | Strict adherence to authorized disclosure; provides targeted hint or probe without leakage. |
| **5. Bengali Clarity** | Unnatural, confusing phrasing, or material translation errors in math terminology. | Understandable with minor friction; slightly awkward phrasing or non-standard math phrasing. | Clear, fluent, natural Bengali appropriate for competitive exam candidates. |
| **6. Pedagogical Usefulness** | Leaves learner stranded; provides no viable cognitive pathway for the next step. | Provides minor support, but does not sufficiently scaffold the next student action. | Effectively scaffolds the student's own discovery and productive struggle. |

---

## Acceptance Thresholds (§13.1, Appx B.3)
An intervention is declared **Accepted** if and only if:
- `Mathematical Correctness == 2`
- `Disclosure Policy == 2`
- `Evidence Alignment >= 1`
- `Pedagogical Usefulness >= 1`
